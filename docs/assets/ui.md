# UI kit assets

All textures are **placeholders**: original art generated by `tools/assets/gen_textures_ui.py` (PIL, deterministic;
run from the repository root). No Star Wars game or film asset, font or logo is used or imitated. The decorative
"notch script" is invented for this mod: stems and notches on a shared baseline, closer to tally marks than to any
alphabet; it must never be redrawn to resemble Aurebesh or any real Star Wars lettering.

Art direction for every piece: Old Republic era hardware, as in the films and games, not Minecraft magic. Weathered
durasteel (cool greys) and brass, chamfered (bevelled) corners, rivets and light scratches around dark translucent
glass; holo-projector accents in Force blue-white (cyan light lines); warm amber only for warnings and Resonance. No
circles, sigils or halos as decoration. Everything must stay readable at GUI scale 1 and crisp at scale 4, so keep
pixel art on whole pixels and keep text areas calm.

Sprites under `textures/gui/sprites/ui/` are stitched into the vanilla GUI atlas and drawn with `blitSprite` as
`oldrepublic:ui/<name>`. Nine-slice sprites carry a `.png.mcmeta` (`{"gui": {"scaling": {"type": "nine_slice", ...}}}`):
the corners are drawn as-is and the edges and centre **tile**, so a final artist must keep anything distinctive
(rivets, brackets, scratches, notches) inside the corner squares and keep the tiled strips uniform.

The HUD also reuses `gui/hud/resonance.png` and `gui/hud/force_push.png` from the quest feature (see `quest.md`).
Bars, rings, brackets, pips, scanlines and banners are drawn in code from the palette in
`client/ui/UiColors.java`, so they stay crisp at every GUI scale.

`gui/container/lightsaber_workbench.png` (saber feature) is no longer drawn: the workbench now uses the kit panel,
sockets and slots.
