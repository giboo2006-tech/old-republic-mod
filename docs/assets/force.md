# Force feature assets (Force Push, the Milestone 2 powers, and Force Sense and Telekinetic Lift)

All sprites are **placeholders generated** by `tools/assets/gen_textures_force.py` (PIL, deterministic). They are original, near-white greyscale with alpha so the game can tint them (dust takes the map colour of the ground). Nothing in this set glows; there are no ground circles, sigils or halos.

Particle textures live in `src/main/resources/assets/oldrepublic/textures/particle/`, sprite lists in `src/main/resources/assets/oldrepublic/particles/`.

Sounds used (owned by the audio brief, not by this file): `force.push.charge`, `force.push.release` (3 variants, pitch ±6% applied in code), `force.push.impact`, `force.push.slam`, `force.fail`.

Debris uses vanilla `minecraft:block` particles of the actual ground/wall/foliage block, so it needs no assets.

Heal reuses the quest feature's `force_mote` particle (recoloured warm in code). Sounds for every power are the lead's placeholder recipes in `tools/assets/sounds/force.py` (ids `force.pull.*`, `force.jump.*`, `force.heal.*`, `force.lightning.*`, plus `saber.lightning_block`); see `docs/AUDIO_BRIEF.md`.

## Milestone 3 sounds (Force Sense and Telekinetic Lift)

Recipes: `tools/assets/sounds/force.py` (W3), rendered with `python3 tools/assets/gen_sounds.py --m3 --feature force` (never `--java`). All files are mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis and **placeholder generated** (synthesised from scratch; no recorded or third-party audio). The file names, `sounds.json`, the subtitles (`lang-parts/sounds.json`) and `ModSounds` are unchanged.

Force Sense's holocron pulses reuse the story sound `story.calling_holocron`, played quietly with the first pulse and louder when the site is closer. Danger Sense's edge tick (`client/hud/DangerEdgeHud`) is drawn in code and needs no texture. Telekinetic Lift's palm air reuses `air_ripple`, its pebbles and dust `gather_dust`, its hurl streak `push_streak`, and its slam debris uses vanilla `minecraft:block` particles of the landing block. Mending Breath's wisps reuse `gather_dust`.
