# Paths and ranks: art and sound (Milestone 5)

Owner: W5. Everything below is original and generated by our own scripts, and every file is a **placeholder**: the Watch
holocron's texture by `tools/assets/gen_textures_turning.py` (PIL, deterministic, its own seed), the three sounds by
`tools/assets/sounds/turning.py` through `python3 tools/assets/gen_sounds.py --m5 --feature turning` (numpy synthesis,
nothing sampled). Nothing copies or imitates a Star Wars film or game asset, icon, effect or sound. The effects are drawn
only with dust, motes, embers and ash (spec 15): no ring, sigil, halo, beam or glow outline, nothing on the ground,
nothing that flashes or shakes the camera. Design: `docs/design/turning.md`.

Regenerate:

```
python3 tools/assets/gen_textures_turning.py
python3 tools/assets/gen_sounds.py --m5 --feature turning
```

## A1. Effects, phase by phase (15.1, 15.3)

Client: `client/quest/turning/TurningFx`. Every effect is a few timed beats run from the client tick; counts go through
the VFX setting (`Vfx.count`) and are skipped out of range (`Vfx.inRange`); a level change or a disconnect drops the
beats still pending. The budgets are those of 15.1 at Standard.

Levels after `gen_sounds.finish`: peaks -2.8 to -2.9 dBFS, mean -18.0 to -18.6 dB (RMS about 0.12), like the M4 banners.
None loops or streams; volume 1.0. The file names are the Phase 0 placeholder names, so `sounds.json` and the subtitles

designer: keep the three as one family (the offer's unresolved interval, the rite's pivot chord, the ceremony's open
fifth), warm and acoustic rather than electronic, no choir and nothing that quotes a film score.

## A4. What the turning uses from others

- **Particles**: W6's `gather_dust` (tinted per effect), W3's `crystal_glint` and `tomb_dust`, W8's `flare_ember`.
- **The Compass**: the M3 Wayfarer's Compass figure (`GatekeeperFigure`), its Pathfinder look (`rank_ceremony`,
  `RANK_PATHFINDER`, sent when the pedestal is used), the sound `holocron.gatekeeper.form` and the portrait
  `gatekeeper_wayfarers_compass` for `rank4_compass`.
- **People**: the messengers are W4's hub people `acolyte_0` to `3` and `apprentice_0` to `2`; the warden is a hub host
  with W4's skin `warden` ("Warden Ostrel"); the prisoners are hub people with W4's skin `republic_prisoner`, named by
  W5's lang. Portraits: `acolyte`, `apprentice` (the offers), `ansa`, `kaddor`, `officer_imperial` (the warden), and the
  mentors' M2 portraits.
- **Rewards**: the robes and the mantle (`jedi_master_robe`, `sith_darth_robe`, `wayfarer_mantle`) are W7's items and
  art; Saelen's crystal is the M2 kyber crystal item, green, attuned to the player, named by W5's lang.
- **Banners**: W4's `lesson_complete` 12 and 13 and their lang.
