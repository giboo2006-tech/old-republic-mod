# World assets: crystal caves and the Trial of Reflection

All textures are **placeholders**, original art generated by `tools/assets/gen_textures_world.py` (PIL, deterministic;
run from the repository root). The structure templates are generated by `tools/assets/gen_structures.py`
(deterministic: running it twice gives byte-identical NBT; Milestone 6: the cave's templates are now made by
`tools/assets/structures/o1/build_all.py`, and `gen_structures.py` skips them). No Star Wars game or film assets are
used or imitated. Sounds are the shared placeholder sounds from `ModSounds` (see `docs/AUDIO_BRIEF.md`); this feature
adds none.

## Brief for the final structure builder

The generated pieces are functional placeholders (Milestone 6: O1 rebuilt them; see the table above). A builder
replacing them in a structure block must keep: the jigsaw blocks (names, targets, pools, orientations listed in
`docs/design/world.md` section 3.3), the template sizes or the cave tests in `WorldGameTests` (update the expected sizes
there), the meditation stone at heart-local `(22, 1, 38)` (`CaveLocator.HEART_STONE_LOCAL`), the resonant seal as the
only way into the trial chamber (radius about 7 around the stone, height 6), the crystal-veined wall as the only way
into the hidden chamber, and the unstable clusters placed so the 2.5-block danger radius covers the walkway.

# Milestone 3: sites, the puzzle kit, resources and decor

Everything below is original art and sound made by our own scripts; nothing is copied from or imitates a Star Wars game or
film. Textures are **placeholders** from `tools/assets/gen_textures_world.py` (PIL, deterministic, seeded per function; the
Milestone 1 textures above stay byte-identical). Block states, block models, item definitions and item models are hand
specified (final unless noted). Sounds are designed stand-ins from `tools/assets/sounds/world.py`, rendered by
`python3 tools/assets/gen_sounds.py --m3 --feature world`. Their file names are the placeholder names, so `sounds.json`
and the subtitles (the lead's files) do not change.

Render layers: in 26.2 a block model picks its chunk layer from its texture's alpha. The urns, the pedestal, the vent,
the lumen moss, the wreck salvage, the terminal and the cairn have cut-out pixels. The stones, panels, plating, seals and
rubble are opaque.

## Site templates and worldgen data (M3)

The 21 site templates of spec 15.1 are **placeholders** generated by `tools/assets/gen_structures_m3.py` (run
`python3 tools/assets/gen_structures_m3.py` from the repository root). (Milestone 6: all 21 were rebuilt, the landmarks
by `tools/assets/structures/o1/build_all.py`, the outposts by `structures/o2/` and the shrines by `structures/qs2/`;
`gen_structures_m3.py` now writes only its three processor lists. The new sizes are noted in the table below.) The
script is deterministic: fixed seeds, the NBT writer and `Template` class of `gen_structures.py` (patched for
`TAG_INT_ARRAY`), a fixed gzip mtime, so a re-run writes byte-identical files. It refuses to write anything when a check
fails:

- every template has its frozen size and exactly its frozen anchors, and every anchor cell holds its real block;
- every puzzle's redstone works, through a small simulation of the vanilla rules (wire power and connections,
  repeaters, strong and weak power, `hasNeighborSignal`). Each Listening Hall counterweight extends exactly its two step
  stones; the urn keeper opens the sanctum seal and readies the Sith pedestal; each shrine's counterweight opens its seal
  and readies its pedestal;
- each structure is assembled on flat ground the way vanilla places rigid pieces. Every expected piece is placed, no two
  piece boxes intersect and every jigsaw opening lines up;
- the resource counts are inside spec 3.2: lumen moss 10–14 per ruin, funerary urns 8–10 on the tomb's main route,
  wreck salvage 6–8 per field.

Template rules (keep them in step with the Java code):

- Coordinates are template-local: x east, y up, z south.
- Runtime templates (outposts, shrines) have their entrance on the local north side (z = 0).
- Unset cells are structure void.
- `buried` B: layers 0..B-1 are underground, layer B-1 is the ground and layer B is the feet layer. Worldgen starts use
  `start_height` 1 − B. Runtime placement (`RuntimeSites`) puts layer B at the stand.
- Anchors are not blocks. They are named cells in a sidecar, and every anchor cell holds its real block:
  - `site_spawner` with its group NBT at `SQUAD`, `SENTINEL_n`, `SCAVENGER_n`, `DIGGERS` and `GUARD`;
  - chests with their loot table at `CHEST_n`, `CACHE`, `DECK_CACHE` and `ANCHOR_LOCKER`;
  - the pedestal at `PEDESTAL*`, with its kind and readiness;
  - the urn keeper with its `urns` offsets, the echo urns, the vents and the counterweights;
  - the bounty terminal at `TERMINAL`;
  - veiled stone at the courtyard's `STAIR_MOUTH` and the processional's `NICHE`, and veiled plating at the hull's `HATCH`;
  - air at the feet cells (`HEART`, `SURFACE_GUARD`, `OFFICER`, `GARRISON_n`, the gate's `STAIR_MOUTH`).
- No template carries entities: people come from the site spawners and the terminal's garrison.
- Puzzle wiring is plain vanilla redstone, written without block updates. Every wire carries the connection properties
  vanilla would compute and power 0.

halo or outline, and nothing flashes. The phase-by-phase treatment (anticipation, activation, travel, impact,
aftermath) of every event and of the site ambience is in `docs/design/world.md`, section 24.
