# Chapter Five and the factions: art and sound (Milestone 5)

Owner: W4. Everything below is original and generated by our own scripts, and every file is a **placeholder**: the
skins, Vessba, the portraits and Ulvaine's writ by `tools/assets/gen_textures_hub.py --m5`, the two faction icons by
`tools/assets/gen_textures_identity.py --m5` (PIL, deterministic, fixed seeds), the two sounds by
`tools/assets/sounds/story_m5.py` through `python3 tools/assets/gen_sounds.py --m5 --feature story_m5` (numpy
synthesis, nothing sampled). Nothing copies or imitates a Star Wars film or game asset, icon, effect or sound: Vessba is
an original Hutt design, the clan's armour is rounded slate-blue plate with a straight visor band, and no faction icon
borrows a film or game emblem. The verse vision is drawn only with a screen wash, text and a faint motif: no ring,
sigil, halo, beam or glow outline, nothing on the ground, nothing that flashes or shakes the camera. Design:
`docs/design/chapter_five.md`.

Regenerate (each writes only its Milestone 5 files with `--m5`):

```
python3 tools/assets/gen_textures_hub.py --m5
python3 tools/assets/gen_textures_identity.py --m5
python3 tools/assets/gen_sounds.py --m5 --feature story_m5
```

## A1. Effects, phase by phase (15.1, 15.3)

### `verse_vision` (client `quest.VerseVision`; sent `toPlayer`, intensity = its ticks, 120 by `chapter_five.visionTicks`)

Drawn last over the HUD (`HudElementRegistry.addLast`), so it washes over everything. The variant is the vision, by
the order the stones fall (not their index): 0 the First Cantor composing the Hymn, 1 the choir of bound singers, 2 the
Hymn sung over a city that fell quiet.

| Phase | Ticks (at 120) | Look | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | 0 | Any earlier vision and its sound stop | `story.verse_vision` as a UI sound (volume 1, pitch 0.9): the world hushes (low air swelling in) |
| Fade in | 0 to 18 | A wash of pale grey-gold (`#D9CDA8`, alpha 0.46) over the whole screen, deeper toward the top and bottom like light through old glass (`#6E6248` gradients over the top and bottom quarters, alpha 0.38 and 0.42 at the edges); with flashing effects on, the wash breathes by 0.04 alpha every 50 ticks | |
| The motif | 0 to the end | Faint ink (`#2E2618`) low on the screen. **0, the stave:** five ruled lines across the middle two thirds, two thirds down; 14 notes written in one by one, left to right, as the vision runs. **1, the choir:** nine kneeling singers along the lower fifth; with flashing on, three motes (`#F6E9C4`) rise off each and fade over 40 px. **2, the city:** a skyline of uneven roofs along the bottom with pale roof lines; its lit windows (`#F2D27A`) go dark one by one, each at its own moment by its place in the street | the choir sings three slow notes of the Hymn over the stone's slow beating hum |
| The lines | from 14, about 32 ticks each | Three subtitled lines (`vision.oldrepublic.verse_<one|two|three>.<1-3>`), one after another, in italic `#F6E9C4` at the middle of the screen on a soft dark band (`#2E2618`, alpha 0.32, fading to nothing above and below); each fades in over 8 ticks, rising 3 px into place, and out over 8 | a faint high glint as each note begins |
| Fade out | 100 to 120 | Everything fades together | the hush lifts |
| End early | any | Death, a level change or a disconnect ends it at once and stops its sound | |

A vision is never shorter than 60 ticks. With flashing effects off, the wash and the motif hold steady (no breathing, no
rising motes). `VerseVision.dipping()` is true while it plays, so W3's ambience can duck under it (15.3).

| Event | File | Length | Design |

The Hymn heard for the first time: beautiful and wrong |
| `story.verse_vision` | `story/verse_vision.ogg` | 6 s + tail (7.20 s) | The vision's bed, as long as the vision: the world hushes (low-passed air swelling in over 0.8 s, holding, lifting over the last 1.4 s); the stone hums (G2 against a sine 0.7 Hz off it, a slow beat, a fifth above, very soft); a distant choir sings three slow descending notes of the Hymn (G4, F4, E-flat 4), each swelling and fading into the next, each a different vowel; a faint high glint as each begins; a long dark reverb |

Levels after `gen_sounds.finish`: peaks -3.0 and -2.9 dBFS, means -16.5 and -16.7 dB, like the M4 banners. Neither
loops or streams; volume 1.0. The file names are the Phase 0 placeholder names, so `sounds.json` is unchanged. The
subtitles are the lead's (`subtitles.oldrepublic.story.chapter_five` "Chapter Five begins", which plays at the chapter's
end: see the report; `story.verse_vision` "A verse echoes"). Brief for a final sound designer: one family for the
chapter (the choir's open, unresolved chord; the stone's inharmonic ring and slow beating), quiet and uncanny rather than
grand, no full orchestra and nothing that quotes a film score; the vision's bed must leave room for the three lines.

## A4. Vessba the Hutt (`textures/entity/vessba/vessba.png`, 128x128; client `vendor.VessbaModel`)

An original design, two blocks tall and almost two wide: a broad, low slug body resting on her dais, a tail tapering in
four segments that curls round to her right, an upright belly-and-chest mass, a wide flat head sitting straight on it
with heavy jowls, two heavy-lidded amber eyes set in the brow, and two short arms folded over the belly. The hide is
mottled sand and ochre (`#B09262` with darker `#846642` blotches, a fixed scatter so the faces agree where they meet) with a paler
ridged belly; the kajidic's gold (`#D6AA48`) at the collar, the wrists and the tail ring; two wine cheek marks
(`#782828`); a wide mouth. In game she breathes (the chest swells a little), turns her head to whoever she looks at,
stirs her tail tip and now and then drums her hands on her belly. The UV layout is in the model's Javadoc and mirrored by
the generator. Status: placeholder generated (the model is final code).

## A8. What the chapter uses from others

- **Effects**: W8's `hologram` (`HologramFx`, Ulvaine's model and the `story.hologram` loop); W3's ambience reads
  `VerseVision.dipping()`.
- **People**: the hub people's body (`MentorModel`, `HubNpcRenderer`) for every variant but Vessba; W8's cartel
  enforcers, Clan Hask warriors and sand scours in the chapter's fights; the scavengers group (M4) at the debtor camp.
- **Places**: W3's Saltgate and holdfast templates and anchors (`NPC_DASK`, `NPC_DEBTOR`, `NPC_VARRA`,
  `COUNTING_HOUSE_1..8`, `SPAWNER`), the debtor camp, the verse stones and the Hymnal.
- **Rewards**: W7's Cantor's Emitter schematic and kyber crystal item, W7's legendary schematics on the vendors, the M4
  Mandalorian set and jet pack, W2's concussion missiles, the M4 credit chits.
- **Banners**: the M2 banner (`Banners`) and the M4 arrival banner; the sounds of W5, W7 and the lead for variants 12
  to 15.
