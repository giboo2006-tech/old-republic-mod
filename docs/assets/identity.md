# Identity assets: Frontier Settler, rope fibre, faction icons, identity sounds

All textures are **placeholders**: original art generated by `tools/assets/gen_textures_identity.py` (PIL,
deterministic; run from the repository root). All sounds are **placeholders** synthesised by the recipes in
`tools/assets/sounds/identity.py` (`python3 tools/assets/gen_sounds.py --feature identity`). No Star Wars game or film
assets are used or imitated.

## Skin layout (`frontier_settler_*.png`)

Standard box layout (each box at `(u, v)` with size `w x h x d`: top and bottom in the first `d` rows, then the
east, front, west and back faces). Mirrors `client.identity.SettlerModel`.

| Part | UV origin | Box size (w x h x d) |
|---|---|---|
| head | 0,0 | 8x8x8 |
| hat (left empty) | 32,0 | 8x8x8 |
| legs (left mirrored) | 0,16 | 4x12x4 |
| body | 16,16 | 8x12x4 |
| arms (left mirrored) | 40,16 | 4x12x4 |
| tunic (over body, 0.3 inflation, hangs 2 px below the waist) | 16,32 | 8x14x4 |
| rolled hood (around the neck) | 0,52 | 9x3x5 |

## Sounds (`tools/assets/sounds/identity.py`)

| Event | Placeholder | Brief for the final sound designer |
|---|---|---|
| `identity.alignment_light` | Soft rising two-note tone | Warm, airy rising chime, under a second; hopeful, never triumphant. |
| `identity.alignment_dark` | Low falling murmur | A low, breathy descending murmur with a little grit; uneasy, not evil-villain. |
| `identity.rank_up` | Swelling chord | A short, solemn swell with a resolving final note; ceremony rather than fanfare. |
| `dialogue.open` | Holo chirp | A quiet holo-projector blip as the panel opens. |
| `dialogue.select` | Click | A short soft click, distinct from vanilla buttons. |
| `settler.hurt` / `settler.death` | Vocal-like grunts | Human pain sounds without words (two or three variants). |
| `settler.freed` | Rope snap plus relieved breath | A rope cut and a relieved exhale; played lower when they were made to pay. |
