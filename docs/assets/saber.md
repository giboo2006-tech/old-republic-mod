# Saber assets: lightsaber, hilt parts, kyber crystal, workbench

All textures are **placeholders**: original art generated by `tools/assets/gen_textures_saber.py` (PIL, deterministic;
the script also writes every model, item definition, blockstate and particle JSON listed below). No Star Wars game or
film assets are used or imitated: hilts are generic cylinders with original detailing, not recognisable film props.
Sounds are the shared placeholder sounds from `ModSounds` (`SABER_*`, `WORKBENCH_SLOT`, `WORKBENCH_ASSEMBLE`; see
`docs/AUDIO_BRIEF.md`); this feature adds no sound files.

Paths are under `src/main/resources/`.

**Final-sound brief, `saber.hum.singing`.** A loop laid over the normal saber hum of a saber built around the singing
crystal, so it must sit above the hum (which lives below 400 Hz) and never mask it. A high, clear crystal tone, as if a
wet finger circled a crystal glass: a fundamental near E6 with a fifth above (B6) and a faint octave, each beating slowly
(1 to 2 Hz) against a partner a hair off pitch, so it shimmers rather than whistles; a breath of air on top; a short
bright room tail. No melody and no attack: it is a layer, steady from the first sample, seamless at the loop point
(any length that loops cleanly, 2 to 6 s). Peak well below the hum (the code plays it at 0.45). It must not read as an
alarm or tinnitus: keep the top end soft, nothing sharp above 8 kHz. Subtitle (already in `lang-parts/sounds.json`): "Singing crystal hums".

The singing crystal's blade has no art of its own: the blade is the normal tinted blade, in `BladeColor.AMBER`
(`color.oldrepublic.blade.amber`) by default.
