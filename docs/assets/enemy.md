# Milestone 3: Republic and Imperial troopers, the frag grenade

Owner: W4. Everything below is original art and sound made by our own scripts; nothing is copied from or imitates a
Star Wars game or film costume, prop or sound. Textures are **placeholders** from `tools/assets/gen_textures_trooper.py`
(the eight trooper skins) and the Milestone 3 part of `tools/assets/gen_textures_enemy.py` (`m3_assets()`: spawn eggs,
the grenade, the four particles). Both are PIL, deterministic and seeded per texture; the Milestone 1 and 2 textures
stay byte-identical. Sounds are designed stand-ins from `tools/assets/sounds/enemy.py` (the M3 recipes), rendered by
`python3 tools/assets/gen_sounds.py --m3 --feature enemy`; their file names are the Phase 0 placeholder names, so
`sounds.json` and the subtitles (the lead's files) do not change.

The trooper skins are composited from the very layers the player's trooper armour uses
(`gen_textures_equipment.trooper_layers`, see `docs/assets/equipment.md`): an undersuit, the leggings layer, then the
armour layer, so a trooper and a player in the same set look alike. A final artist should change the armour and the
skins together.
