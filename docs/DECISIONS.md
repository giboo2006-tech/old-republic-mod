# Decisions and lore departures

A record of choices that shape the mod, so later work stays consistent. Newest last.

## Platform and project

- **Minecraft 26.2 on Fabric.** Loader 0.19.5, Fabric API 0.161.0+26.2, Java 25, official Mojang names. Library features that do not exist on 26.2 are blocking decisions for the owner, not silently worked around.
- **Fresh codebase.** The earlier "galaxyfaraway" mod was not ported; ideas are rebuilt to this project's standards.
- **Display name** "Star Wars: Echoes of the Old Republic", mod id `oldrepublic`.
- **Server-authoritative.** Clients send intents only (key presses, menu buttons). All damage, costs, cooldowns, rewards and quest state are decided on the server.
- **Compact effect events.** One small packet per gameplay moment (`EffectEventPayload`); every client builds particles, sounds, light and shake locally from it, scaled by its own quality preset. No per-particle networking.
- **Data-driven where it helps.** Balance numbers live in `config/oldrepublic/balance.json` (server side, written with defaults on first start). Structures, loot tables, tags, damage types and recipes are data files.
- **Original assets only.** Every texture, model, sound and structure is generated by scripts in `tools/assets/` and labelled placeholder until replaced by final art. Nothing is copied from films, games or other mods.
- **Testing.** Builds run on GitHub Actions. `./gradlew build` runs server game tests; a separate job runs a client game test that boots the game, joins a world, exercises features and prints screenshots. A dedicated-server start is part of the server game tests.
- **Per-feature ownership.** Each feature owns its package, lang part (`src/main/lang-parts/<feature>.json`), mixin config and assets, and talks to other features through small shared contracts (`QuestHooks`, `CombatHooks`, `EffectEvents`, `ModComponents`, `CaveLocator`).

## Art direction

- Force effects read as bent air, dust, debris, pressure and light, never as ground circles, sigils, halos or "magic" glyphs.
- Enemy attack tells are never hidden or reduced by quality presets.
- Screen shake and flashing have their own settings, independent of the quality preset. Shake defaults to 60%.
- Placeholder palette: weathered durasteel greys, sand and worn cloth, deep blue and red accents; kyber glows white with a colour tint.

## Lore departures (gameplay adaptations)

- **Midi-chlorian Resonance** is a visible, slowly rising number that grows only from major milestones (Awakening, first Force Push, the Trial, the first lightsaber). In canon a midi-chlorian count is innate and fixed; here it represents how strongly the character resonates with the Force, as asked for in the design brief.
- **Force Attunement Level and XP** are a game abstraction for training and experience.
- **Kyber in a frontier cave.** Crystals are found in caves on an unnamed frontier world rather than Ilum or another named world; many worlds hide veins.
- **No master for the first saber.** A Force-sensitive wanderer finds a crystal and builds a saber alone. It is framed as the crystal choosing them and as a rough first blade; finding a teacher is the next chapter.
- **The Trial of Reflection and the Shadow Echo** are original, inspired by cave trials in the wider canon, not a canonical ritual.
- **Crystal poachers** as a mercenary crew are original.
- **Blade colour** is a free choice at the workbench and purely cosmetic in Milestone 1. Crystal colour lore (and any colour-linked mechanics) can come later.

## Milestone 2: Identity and Builds

Spec: `docs/MILESTONE_2.md`. Recorded here as built.

### Scope and design

- **Path choice is final in M2.** Jedi ↔ Sith changes only through `/oldrepublic identity path <player> <path> force`. Drifting alignment has consequences instead (dormant passives, opposed or refused side powers, blocked trials, Atone / Embrace). Redemption and corruption questlines are left for M3 (spec open question 1; the spec's recommended default, built as such; no different answer from the owner is recorded).
- **Independents** get the `unbound` passive (Universal powers ×0.85 cost, ×0.9 cooldown), the same mission, +3 SP instead of +1, slot 4 and the `first_rank` Resonance milestone ("No Master"). An Independent may join a mentor once, later. Their own signature power is left for M3 (open question 2, default built).
- **Heavy attack is Sneak + Attack**, the lunge Sprint + Attack (open question 3, default built). A hold-to-charge option can follow if play-testing asks for it.
- **Story structures are placed into existing worlds**, on natural ground only (no built blocks within the clearance, outside spawn protection, terrain adapted, nothing non-natural removed). `story.placeStructures=false` keeps it to entities only (open question 4, default built).
- **Alignment is separate from path**, moves only through one-time story choices, dialogue stances, Atone / Embrace and capped contextual acts, never through ordinary ability use, and does not drift over time.
- **Four ability slots (R, Z, V, B), no radial Force wheel.** At most five actives exist in M2, so one stays unslotted on purpose. The slot 1 binding keeps the M1 id `key.oldrepublic.use_force_power`.
- **The Push branch choice moved into the skill tree.** `ForceData.pushBranch` stays as a mirror written only by the skill service, so saves keep working; M1 saves are migrated on join. `ChooseBranchPayload`, `BranchChoice`, `PushInfoPayload` and the `force_push.branch*` balance keys were removed.
- **Skill trees and dialogues are data** (`data/oldrepublic/skill_tree/`, `data/oldrepublic/dialogue/`), loaded on start and `/reload` and synced to clients. Numbers shown in tooltips come from the server's balance through `ability_info`.
- **No power changes terrain in M2.** Powers affect other players only with their `pvpEffects` flag and server PvP on, never teammates; Shared Light heals allies without any flag.
- **Story NPCs are per player and owned.** Others can see a player's mentors, Brenn and encounter, but only the owner talks and decides; anyone's kills count for the owner. Mentors are invulnerable except to `/kill` and the void.
- **Spars carry no death risk** on either side and are kept in memory only.
- **Spawn protection:** 26.2 exposes no getter for the server's spawn-protection radius, so story sites use `story.spawnProtectionRadius` (16) from the balance file instead of `server.properties`.
- **Two mentor colours** (Jedi green, Sith red) are cosmetic, as blade colour was in M1.

### Phase 0 contract notes (frozen)

From the spec's "Phase 0 notes (as built)":

- `IdentityEvents` are `public static final Event<...>` fields built with `EventFactory.createArrayBacked`, with nested callbacks `AlignmentChanged#onAlignmentChanged(ServerPlayer, int before, int after)` and `PathChanged#onPathChanged(ServerPlayer, Path before, Path after)`, fired after the value is stored.

- Stat modifier counters in `ForceData.counters`: `mod_max_energy` (flat FE), `mod_max_energy_pct` (**added** for `calm_focus`, applied after every flat bonus), `mod_regen_pct`, `mod_regen_delay` (never below 0). An absent key changes nothing; the skill service writes them and calls `Progression.commit`.
- `Banners.show(title, subtitle, accent)` queues into `FlourishHud` (at most 4 waiting, extras dropped); M1 level and Resonance banners share the queue.
- The Standing tab is W1's `StandingPanel` (`render(GuiGraphicsExtractor, Font, x, y, w, h, partialTick)`, `mouseClicked(MouseButtonEvent, boolean)`), hosted by W3's Force screen. UI kit additions: `TabStrip`, `NodeSocket`, `PortraitFrame`; portraits are GUI sprites.
- `ClientConfig.showMinorAlignmentToasts` (default true), toggled with `/oldrepublicfx alignment_toasts on|off`.
- Sounds: `tools/assets/sounds/events_m2.json` lists every M2 event; each feature owns a recipe module `tools/assets/sounds/<feature>.py`; `gen_sounds.py --feature <name>` / `--m2` renders them. Every id has a `ModSounds` constant (dots become underscores).
- Block tags `story_ground` and `built_blocks` use optional entries, so a missing referenced tag never breaks loading. Empty mixin configs `oldrepublic-identity.mixins.json` and `oldrepublic-skill.mixins.json`. Lang parts are created by the features that need them (`mergeLang` merges whatever exists).

### Lore departures and notes (spec section 17)

- **Era: the cold peace** (recorded here in M2 as "about 3,640 BBY"; corrected in Milestone 3 to about 3,650 to 3,645 BBY, see the Milestone 3 lore notes). A Jedi Watchwoman (the Watchmen are an Old Republic Jedi role) guards this frontier sector; an Imperial Sith Overseer scouts it for Force-sensitives to send to the Empire. Neither may openly fight on neutral ground because of the Treaty of Coruscant, which is why the Crossing is a standoff.
- **Adult ranks.** In canon, Jedi Initiates are usually children taken young. Here an adult wanderer is accepted as an Initiate (Sith: Acolyte) at the Crossing and becomes a Padawan or Apprentice after a trial. This is a gameplay adaptation.
- **Original characters:** Master Ilyra Saelen, Overseer Teyvar Sorn, Acolyte Brenn Ostrak and Captain Hessa Dray are original. No names are taken from the Old Republic games.
- **Saber forms** Soresu and Ataru are lore form names used for their known character (defence; acrobatic aggression). Their numbers are gameplay.
- **Alignment as a number** from -100 to +100 is a game abstraction of the brief's light/dark consequences.
- **Lightning colour** is white with violet-blue edges, consistent with the films and games, and chosen for readability, not copied from any asset.
- **Factions in M2** are the Jedi Order, the Sith Empire and the Frontier Settlers. The Republic and the Mandalorians come later; the poacher crew has no reputation.

## Milestone 3: Exploration

Spec: `docs/MILESTONE_3.md`. Recorded in Phase 0; the as-built notes from integration are under "As built (2026-10-17)".

### The owner's decisions (Gabriel)

- **Era armour moves from Milestone 4 into Milestone 3.** The brief's roadmap puts armour in Milestone 4 ("Additional weapons and armour"). Gabriel moved four wearable sets into M3: Republic trooper armour, Sith (Imperial) trooper armour, Jedi robes and Sith robes. Each set has four pieces, each piece has traits, and a full set adds a bonus (spec 9). The rest of the Milestone 4 armour work (further sets, upgrades and mods) stays in Milestone 4.
- **Two trooper armies.** Republic troopers and Sith (Imperial) troopers are both Milestone 3 mobs. They share one entity class, with the roles Rifleman, Field Medic and Sergeant (Republic) and Rifleman, Grenadier and Sergeant (Imperial), plus non-combat officers (spec 7).
- **Robes for both Orders.** Jedi robes and Sith robes are wearable gear with stats, not costume items. The rank 3 Knight and Lord robes are alternative chest pieces of these sets.
- **Chapter Two pacing stays as it is.** The Trial of the Blade still needs level 6, and Chapter Two XP is unchanged. A straight Jedi run reaches the debrief at level 5, so the player gains a level before asking for the Trial (`KNOWN_ISSUES.md`). A raise of the Chapter Two story XP was tried and reverted.

### Scope and design (spec defaults)

Each open question in spec section 19 has a default, and work goes ahead on it. The owner's answers are still pending (see "Open questions for the owner").

- **The first major boss is Orren Vasch, the Collector** (question 1): a fallen Jedi Archivist, fought in three phases in his Reliquary. He ties the ruins, tombs, holocrons and both armies together. A Sith Lord boss is kept for the Milestone 4 enclave and academy.
- **Redemption and corruption move to Milestone 4** (question 2), where enclave and academy locations exist to set them in. Milestone 2 had already deferred them to M3. In M3 the alignment choices are the opposed-side holocrons (Seal, Break or Listen) and the boss ending. The operator command stays the only way to change path, and a late mentor join is refused once Chapter Three has begun.
- **Rank 3 ships in Milestone 3** (question 3): Jedi Knight, Sith Lord and the Independent Pathfinder title. If playtesting shows the bar is too high, the lead lowers the `chapter_three.rank3*` config values rather than cutting the ceremony.
- **Telekinetic Lift is the Independent signature power in M3** (question 4), although the brief lists it as Universal. That way the Independent path has a power of its own in the boss fight. Jedi and Sith can get Lift in Milestone 4 through one node requirement, with no code change.
- **Force Sense is Universal** and has its own key, not an ability slot: H by default, or U if the Phase 0 probe finds H taken by vanilla 26.2 (spec 11.1).
- **Puzzle blocks never move.** Force-reactive blocks change state and drive redstone hidden in the walls. Every primary route needs only Push and Sense; Pull, Force Jump and Lift open optional caches. Puzzles reset, but broken decor is not rebuilt.
- **Outposts are shared, other sites are per player.** The two outposts come from worldgen. Where none is near a player, one is placed at runtime, shared, and recorded in a world-level attachment (`WorldSites`). Courier's Fall, Echo Shrines, mission camps and the Reliquary are per player.
- **Not in M3, by design:** voice acting (barks are subtitled lines with a radio click), companions, PvP faction warfare, rebuilding of griefed structures, and quest party sharing.
- **Cut order** (spec section 1): uniform disguise first, then the Lift branches, natural patrols, the rematch and Unchained mode, robe custom models, the Wreck Field and the Independent bot run. The lead records any cut here and in `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.

### Phase 0 tooling and data (as built)

- **Each lang key belongs to one part.** `mergeLang` now fails the build when a key is defined in two lang parts, even with the same value. No existing key was in two parts when the rule came in, so there is no exception list.
- **Sound events.** `tools/assets/sounds/events_m3.json` lists the 121 new sound ids of spec 16, each with its feature, subtitle, `loop`, `stream` and `volume` (and `variants` for `site.discovered`).
  - `gen_sounds.py` writes `stream` from the list. Without it, the old rule stays: a loop longer than 4 s streams. The five ambience beds, the Chapter Three dream bed and the three boss music loops stream.
  - Until a recipe module defines an M3 id, `gen_sounds.py` renders a labelled generic placeholder for it into `<group>/<rest of the id, dots as underscores>`, numbered when it has variants. Real recipes keep those names.
  - `--m3` renders only the M3 files; `--placeholders` lists the ids still on placeholder audio.
- **`ModSounds.holder(SoundEvent)`** returns `BuiltInRegistries.SOUND_EVENT.wrapAsHolder(sound)`. Every constant is registered, so this is the registry's reference holder.
- **Id check.** `tools/check_m3_ids.py` compares against the pre-Milestone 3 tree (commit `fa82dc8`), frozen inside the script, so it needs no git history. It runs in the CI job `m3-checks`.
- **Ownership.** `tools/ownership_m3.json` is the machine-readable copy of spec 15.2. `tools/check_ownership.py` fails a worker branch (`m3/w<N>-...`) that touches files outside the worker's globs since the tag `m3-phase0`. The lead puts that tag on the Phase 0 commit. Assets of inherited M1 and M2 content follow the package that owns them now.
- **Tags.** Every M3 id that is not registered yet is an optional entry.
  - `mineable/pickaxe` lists the 12 decor blocks and `wreck_salvage`.
  - The vanilla `enchantable/{head,chest,leg,foot}_armor` tags list the 18 armour pieces by slot, and `enchantable/armor`, `enchantable/equippable` and `enchantable/durability` list all 18 (`armor` already includes the slot tags; the spec lists it, and a repeated entry is harmless).
  - Each structure tag lists its structure. The `has_structure/*` biome tags start empty (W1 fills them), and so do the cloth and repair tags (W4 fills them).

### Lore departures and notes (spec section 18)

- **Era: the cold peace, a few years after the Treaty of Coruscant (about 3,650 to 3,645 BBY).** The Republic and the Empire are not at war. Their troopers patrol a treaty line across this frontier and do not fire first. This is why both armies can share a region with the player, and why an "incident" is a story event, not a battle.
  - Checked against Wookieepedia (Legends): the Treaty of Coruscant was signed in 3653 BBY, and the cold war it began lasted until open war resumed in 3642 BBY.
  - The Milestone 2 note "about 3,640 BBY" therefore fell after the cold peace had ended, and is corrected here.
  - No in-game text shows the date.
- **The Watch Archive and the Archivists** are original. The Watch enclave on this frontier and its Archivists fit the canon idea of Jedi who keep records and holocrons, without naming any canon place or order. The Order mints no money: Vasch pays in Republic credit chits stamped with the Archive's vault seal (relief funds the Watch held).
- **Holocrons and gatekeepers** are canon concepts: a holocron holds a recorded teacher, the gatekeeper, who is projected from the device and answers the worthy. Jedi holocrons are cubes and Sith holocrons pyramids, following canon convention, but every model and texture is original. **Wayfarer holocrons** (brass spheres that belong to no Order) are original to this mod.
- **Original characters:** Orren Vasch; the gatekeepers Tessaly Arn, Pell of the Long Road, Osk Brevan, Maelis Dunn, Aveth Morrane, Ione Sarrow, Ruan Ketterin, Sela Vanterre, Hadrek Zarvos, Ysolde the Unbowed, Calix Thrane and Ilvane Torr; the twelve outpost officers (spec 3.5); Rell Okkar, Dovan Lisk and Aldo Brannick.
  - The Sith founder was renamed from "Veskar Mor", so that no two key names (Morrane, Vasch) can be confused in subtitles.
  - The lead checks every name against the canon wikis before release and renames any clash.
- **Trooper looks** are original: Republic off-white plate with navy trim, Imperial charcoal plate with crimson trim. No helmet copies a film or game design.
- **Knight and Lord.** The Knighting and the Naming are adapted for an adult player, as the M2 ranks were.
  - In canon the Council confers Knighthood. Here a single Master acts for the Watch on a distant frontier.
  - An Overseer ranks below a Lord, so Sorn does not make the player a Lord himself. He reads a sealed writ from an unnamed Darth.
- **The rank 3 trial.** The brief asks that ranks need missions and trials. The Collector fight is the rank 3 trial, and the contracts and story missions are the missions (spec 5.8). There is no separate trial duel.
- **Telekinetic Lift** is listed under Universal powers in the brief. M3 makes it the Independent signature, granted by `wayfarer_pell` to Independents only, so the Independent path has a power of its own; the rubble caches are an Independent perk. Jedi and Sith may get Lift in M4 when the Universal set grows.
- **Bolt colours** are chosen for readability: Republic blue-white, Imperial red, poachers orange, sentinel lance amber-white.

### As built (2026-10-17)

Recorded by the lead at integration, from the five worker reports and the lead's fix commits.

**Cuts and scope**
- **The uniform disguise (spec 9.5) is cut (2026-10-17).** It was first on the cut order. `ArmourSets.disguise` always returns NONE, because `Allegiances.of(player)` reads it and a live disguise would make every trooper bolt pass through the wearer (`canHitEntity` skips friendly targets). `equipment.disguiseBreakTicks` and `disguiseBreakRange` stay in the file, unused. No other cut was used: the Lift branches, natural patrols, the rematch and Unchained mode, robe custom models and the Wreck Field all shipped. The Independent bot run (cut 7) is not written.
- **The holocron restore day is game time (2026-10-17; W3 review finding 10, rejected).** The restore is once per `gameTime / restoreCooldownTicks` (24,000), not per `dayTime`. Game time / 24,000 is the project's in-game day index everywhere (`IdentityRules.dayIndex`, `QuestRules.dayIndex`, `MissionService`, `ChapterThreeService`). Spec 4.3 says "once per 24,000 ticks" through the key `restoreCooldownTicks`, which `dayTime` would ignore, and a bed would skip the cooldown. So sleep does not reset it (`KNOWN_ISSUES.md`).

**Combat and Force**
- **NPC heavy attacks stay parryable (2026-10-17).** The frozen `Strike.npcHeavy` contract and the Milestone 2 Captain's parry stagger both make an NPC heavy swing parryable, so the duelist profile's heavy last swing (spec 7.8) is parryable too. W5's `SaberGameTests.duelistProfileCompatibilityConstructor` was aligned to that: it now asserts that the heavy strike is not unparryable and is parried inside the window (`cb2b828`).
- **The NPC deflection cone is read as either side of facing (2026-10-17; W5 review finding F12, accepted).** "Within 70°" (spec 10.7) is read as 70° either side of facing, a 140° arc: `SaberGuard` checks `inFront(..., cone * 2)`. This matches how the code reads the 15° spread and the Reliquary emitter's 12°. The lead accepts W5's reading. For a 70° full arc, set `saber.npcDeflectConeDegrees` (and `collector.deflectConeDegrees` for Vasch) to 35.
- **Lift's Suspend ally rule follows Shared Light (2026-10-17; W3 review finding 4).** Suspend's ally bonus goes to the caster, to players with no PvP damage with the caster within `force_heal.combatExcludeTicks`, and to creatures `Attitudes` reports as ALLIED. The reviewer's `ForcePowers.canAffect` test would deny the bonus to every co-op friend whenever server PvP is on, the vanilla default.
- **One Sense ambience dip (2026-10-17; W3 review finding 11).** W1's `SiteClient.senseDip` dips the site and cave beds by 30% for 20 ticks on the caster's own `force_sense` event. W3's `SenseDuck` was an unused duplicate and is deleted.
- **A holocron moment has a server limit (2026-10-17; W3 review finding 7).** Spec 4.4 sets none. `HolocronAttunement.MAX_MOMENT_TICKS` (2,400, the client watchdog) ends a moment, so an idle player cannot hold a shared pedestal for good. A second player at a busy pedestal gets "Another listens to it now."
- **Force Sense uses H (2026-10-17).** The default key is H (`ForceKeys.FORCE_SENSE`); the fallback U was not needed. The Milestone 2 key-clash check in the client test walks every key mapping, so it covers H.

- **A mission camp's forward fallback checks for other sites (2026-10-17).** Spec 5.4 places camps on the mission rings; the forward fallback after repeated failures used to stamp the camp anywhere. It now tries close rings (16 to 40 blocks) with the 3.6 checks first, and never stamps over a worldgen piece, a recorded site or a build: the mission runs around the bare spot instead. Mission code looks up only its own camp (`1e8eabb`).
- **Pull reaches a pylon crystal by its centre or its tip (2026-10-17).** Spec 8.4 has Pull rip out a focus crystal "within 12 blocks, line of sight". The server read line of sight as a clear line to the crystal's centre, which the pylon block hides from the floor below a gallery even when the crystal shows. It now also accepts a clear line to a point 0.1 below the crystal's top (`PylonFocus.TIP_INSET`, `b380712`).

**Balance keys**
- **Troopers and Force Heal read the mission keys (2026-10-17).** Troopers read `missions.tendTicks`, `escortDespawnTicks`, `interceptYield` and `borderYield`, and Force Heal's revive range is `missions.healTendRange`, in place of W4's stand-in copies under `trooper` and W3's constant 4 (`9f9cccd`). One value lives in one place (`BALANCE.md`).
- **The Echo Shrines and the Reliquary read their rings from `ChapterThreeService.balance()` (2026-10-17).** `EchoShrines.place` uses `chapter_three.shrineRings` / `shrineFallback`, and `ReliquarySites.place` uses `reliquaryRings` / `reliquaryFallback`, capped at 144. This replaces W1's interim read of the section.
- **The rematch emitter has one source (2026-10-17).** It comes only from `collector.emitterChance` (0.25) with `emitterPity` 4. The `echo_cache` table's own emitter entry is never given.
- **The client draws default thresholds (2026-10-17; W2 review finding 13).** The Standing tab's rank 3 checklist, the Missions tab and the Holocrons tab's carry line use the code defaults. Sending the server's balance would need a payload outside the spec 16 table, so it waits; the server's `rank3:ready` decides.

**World and story**
- **The cave locator answers the nearest cave (2026-10-17).** Vanilla `findNearestMapStructure` returns the first cave of the first ring of placement regions that has one, so a player standing in a cave's camp just across a region border was sent to a cave 300 blocks away. `world.CaveLocator` answers the cave whose pieces hold the player, and otherwise searches each placement region that could hold a nearer cave than the vanilla answer (`5ef0bbd`).
- **An open veil keeps the spec's reset window (2026-10-17; W1 review finding 5, rejected).** The reviewer found that an open veil could close early. The lead rejects the finding, for W1's reasons:
  - the 200-tick idle step of spec 3.3 already puts every resettable block's reset 1,000 to 1,200 idle ticks after the last player leaves;
  - the leftover REVEALED tick only shifts the veil's step phase; the reset still needs six idle steps, so it lands in the same window, and the reviewer's 1,040 case is inside it;
  - the suggested fix would make the veil never close: `age` leaves 0 only through an idle step, so treating every OPEN state with age 0 as an arming tick would re-arm forever.
- **The chief's and the fence's yields follow the Milestone 2 rules (2026-10-17).** When the R2 chief or the I1 fence yields, an execution moves no alignment, as with the Milestone 2 yields.
- **Site separation covers Chapter Three (2026-10-17).** The spec 3.6 separation list (`story.siteMinSeparation`, 48) now includes the Chapter Three sites and active mission camps, for the Milestone 2 placements too.
- **Story choice shifts apply before the choice is recorded (2026-10-17).** A major alignment shift is refused once its source id is a recorded choice, so the story missions and the Collector's endings apply the shift first (`7fc0d9c`). The hoard choice is kept only in `HolocronData`, as spec 4.8 has it; the report reads it from there.

### Lore departures and notes (spec section 18)

- **Planets are original.** The brief names Tython-, Korriban-, Dromund Kaas- and Tatooine-inspired places. M4 uses two original worlds instead, so the mod never contradicts canon geography: **Selvaren** (a highland forest world where the Watch began; Tython-like in feel) and **Varkhast** (a red-rock tomb world where the Empire trains acolytes; Korriban-like in feel).
